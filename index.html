About the maker

Stitch by stitch

I'm Valeriya. I make clothes the slow way: I choose prints that belong together, cut them into small pieces and sew them back into one fabric. Then I quilt it, line it and finish every seam by hand.

Patchwork lets old-fashioned florals look new again. Blue and blush, coral and cream, lilac and sage. I pair them the way you'd put together flowers for a bouquet.

Every order starts with a conversation. We talk about what you'll wear it with, take your measurements, and I send you the fabrics before I cut anything.
